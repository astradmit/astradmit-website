What we provide

Astradmit provides personalized 1:1 mentoring and process guidance for MBA and master's aspirants, including profile evaluation, planning, exam-planning oversight, school selection, application-process guidance and interview preparation.

What we do not do

  • Astradmit provides mentoring and process guidance. We do not write essays or recommendation letters.
  • You own your accounts, approve bookings and submit your own applications.
  • Specialist test tuition, exam fees and application fees are separate unless explicitly included.
  • Admission, scholarships, test scores and employment outcomes are not guaranteed.
  • Our support runs from initial exploration through admissions decisions.

Specialists and third parties

When you need focused help, we may help you connect with specialist mentors, industry veterans or established learning providers. Their services are provided by them, under their own terms and fees, unless we state otherwise.
