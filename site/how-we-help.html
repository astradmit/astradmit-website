What we can work on together

Personalized mentoring that brings honest profile evaluation, practical planning, regular check-ins and candid feedback together, so you always have a clear next step.

Area 1

Career Direction & Profile Evaluation

Understand where you stand, and what you are working toward.

We start with your background, strengths, interests and career ambitions to understand whether an MBA or master's degree is the right next step, which programs deserve consideration, and what needs attention before you apply.

What you leave with: A written profile assessment, an initial direction and a prioritized list of next steps.

What we work on

  • Clarifying short- and long-term career goals.
  • Exploring degree and specialization options that support those goals.
  • Evaluating academic background, professional experience, achievements and test readiness.
  • Identifying strengths, gaps and areas requiring further exploration.
  • Reviewing program and career information to connect education choices with realistic opportunities.

Area 2

Personalized Roadmap & Profile Development

Build a realistic plan around your goals, responsibilities and starting point.

We turn your aspirations into a practical roadmap, covering when to apply, what to strengthen and how to use your preparation time meaningfully.

What you leave with: A personalized timeline, profile-development priorities and a shared progress tracker.

What we work on

  • Choosing an appropriate application cycle and target intake.
  • Mapping preparation and application milestones backward from deadlines.
  • Identifying relevant academic learning or skill-development needs.
  • Planning meaningful professional, leadership or extracurricular development where appropriate.
  • Balancing preparation with work, study, finances and personal commitments.
  • Reviewing progress periodically and adjusting the plan when circumstances change.

Area 3

Exam Planning & Preparation Oversight

Choose the right test and prepare with a clear purpose.

We help you make informed testing decisions and organize preparation around your target programs, current readiness and application timeline.

What you leave with: A test-selection rationale, preparation schedule, progress checkpoints and booking plan.

What we work on

  • Comparing GMAT and GRE options against your strengths and target-program requirements.
  • Setting a preparation schedule and identifying suitable learning resources or specialist tutors.
  • Reviewing mock-test progress and preparation consistency.
  • Planning test dates, booking windows and potential retakes.
  • Guiding you through registration requirements while you retain control of your account and booking.
  • Reassessing the application timeline if test readiness changes.

Area 4

School Selection, Application Guidance & Interview Preparation

Make thoughtful choices and approach each application with preparation.

We help you build a considered school list, understand application requirements, and prepare to communicate your goals and experiences clearly, while keeping your application your own.

What you leave with: A school-comparison shortlist, application tracker, preparation checklist and interview feedback.

What we work on

  • Comparing programs by career relevance, curriculum, location, cost, eligibility and candidate fit.
  • Building a balanced shortlist, with candid discussion of competitiveness and uncertainty.
  • Selecting application rounds and organizing school-specific deadlines.
  • Mapping document requirements, recommender timelines and submission tasks.
  • Helping you reflect on your experiences and clarify your application direction.
  • Practicing interviews and providing specific feedback on content, structure and delivery.
  • Discussing offers and trade-offs when decisions arrive.

Clear boundaries

What we deliver, and what we can't promise

We believe in being upfront from the start, so you always know exactly what to expect.

  • Astradmit provides mentoring and process guidance. We do not write essays or recommendation letters.
  • You own your accounts, approve bookings and submit your own applications.
  • Specialist test tuition, exam fees and application fees are separate unless explicitly included.
  • Admission, scholarships, test scores and employment outcomes are not guaranteed.
  • Our support runs from initial exploration through admissions decisions.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Get in touch and we'll be glad to help.

Will you write my essays or recommendation letters?

No. We guide your thinking and preparation, and give feedback on direction, but your application stays your own work.

Can you guarantee admission or a scholarship?

No. Admission, scholarships, test scores and employment outcomes depend on many factors outside anyone's control, so we never promise them. What we promise is honest feedback and a clear plan.

Do you run coaching classes for GMAT or GRE?

We do not run in-house coaching. We help you choose the right test and plan your preparation, and when you need focused tuition we help you connect with relevant specialists and established learning providers.

Are test tuition and application fees included?

Specialist test tuition, exam fees and application fees are separate unless explicitly included in the option you choose.

Do I need a perfect profile to start?

No. You do not need a perfect profile or every answer figured out. We start with an honest conversation about your goals, your options and what comes next.

How long does the support last?

Support runs from your initial exploration through admissions decisions.
