About Astradmit

Cut through the noise and see where you stand

Planning an MBA or master's degree can feel overwhelming. There is plenty of information, but knowing what applies to you, and whom to trust, is not always easy.

Astradmit helps you cut through that noise. Through personalized 1:1 mentoring, honest feedback and practical planning, we help you understand where you stand, explore your options and move forward with a clear plan.

Why Astradmit

Advice that stays grounded and personal

Experience that keeps advice grounded

More than a decade of experience in this field, working alongside leading industry professionals and evaluating thousands of candidate profiles.

Our experience is in helping candidates assess their strengths, recognize gaps and make informed choices, not in claiming credit for their admissions.

One consistent mentor

Our biggest difference is continuity: a mentoring partner who keeps your overall goals in focus from your first questions through your admissions decisions.

We take the time to understand your goals, responsibilities, timeline and budget, because a good recommendation should fit your life, not just look impressive on paper.

The right experts, when you need them

We do not run in-house coaching or pretend one person can be an expert in everything. When you need focused help, we connect you with relevant industry veterans, specialist mentors and established learning providers.

The aim is better guidance, not more services or unnecessary spending.

How we work with you

A clear process, with you in control

Every mentoring relationship follows the same simple pattern, shaped around your goals, your timeline and your budget.

See the full approach

  1. Start with an honest assessment

    Understand your strengths, gaps and options without false reassurance.

  2. Build a plan around you

    Bring school choices, exam preparation, profile development and application timelines into one realistic roadmap.

  3. Stay connected

    Use regular check-ins and candid feedback to review progress and adjust your next steps.

  4. Bring in relevant expertise

    Find specialist support when it adds value, with your needs and budget in mind.

  5. Keep you in control

    We guide your thinking and preparation. We do not write your essays or guarantee admissions. Your application, and your decisions, remain yours.
