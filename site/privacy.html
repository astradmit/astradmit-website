Privacy policy

How Astradmit collects, uses and protects your personal information, including how we use cookies and analytics.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

Astradmit ("we", "us") provides mentoring and process guidance for MBA and Master's aspirants. You can contact us at priyanka@astradmit.com.

Information we collect

When you contact us or request a consultation, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, time zone, current role, study plans, preferred dates and the message you write. If you become a mentoring client, we will also receive information about your education, work experience and goals so that we can advise you.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, arrange and hold consultations, provide mentoring, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run our website and communicate with you. They process information only on our behalf.

  • FormSubmit delivers the details you enter on our contact form to our email inbox.
  • Google Firebase hosts this website.
  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics measure how the website is used, but only if you accept analytics cookies (see below).
  • YouTube plays videos on our Resources pages, but only after you press play.

Some of these providers may process information outside India. If we introduce you to a specialist mentor or learning provider, we will do so only with your agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent. Email us at priyanka@astradmit.com and we will respond.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, for example which pages are viewed, how people arrive, and what type of device and browser they use. These tools use cookies, which are small text files stored on your device.

Your choice. When you first visit, we ask whether you accept analytics cookies. We turn analytics cookies on only if you click Accept. If you decline, or have not yet chosen, analytics cookies are not set. In that case Google's tools may still receive limited technical information, such as the page viewed and your browser type, without cookies.

Changing your mind. You can change your choice at any time using , here or in the website footer. We remember your choice in your browser for up to 12 months.

Other cookies. Our contact form does not set cookies. If you press play on a YouTube video, YouTube (Google) may set its own cookies while you watch. Links to our Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube pages lead to third-party sites with their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
