About Astradmit: honest, personal MBA and Master's mentoring

Honest guidance. Personal mentoring. A clearer path forward.

In short: Astradmit is a one-to-one mentoring service for MBA and Master's applicants, founded by Priyanka Khandelwal. She has spent 12 years helping applicants understand where they stand and build stronger applications. Astradmit gives honest feedback and structured guidance. It does not write essays or promise admission outcomes.

Our approach

Cut through the noise

There is plenty of information about MBAs and Master's degrees, but knowing what applies to you, and whom to trust, is not always easy.

Through personalized 1:1 mentoring, honest feedback and practical planning, we help you understand where you stand, explore your options and move forward with a clear plan.

Founder

Meet the Founder

I'm Priyanka Khandelwal, founder of Astradmit.

Over the past 12 years, I have helped thousands of MBA and Master's applicants. My work focuses on helping candidates understand where they realistically stand, identify the right programs, and build a stronger, more strategic application.

Applying abroad often involves many decisions and many service providers. Candidates may invest in test preparation, profile building, essay support, and interview coaching without always knowing what they need, when they need it, or whether it will genuinely add value.

Astradmit was created to change that. We connect candidates with the right expert or specialist at the right stage of their journey, so they can make informed decisions rather than spending time and money on trial and error.

Our approach is direct and practical. We assess your profile honestly, help you identify strengths and gaps, and guide you through school selection, application planning, and profile development. We do not write application essays or promise admission outcomes. Instead, we provide one-to-one mentoring, candid feedback, and structured guidance so that you can present your strongest, most authentic application.

Astradmit was built on a simple belief: good guidance does not tell you what you want to hear. It helps you understand your options, choose the right support, and move forward with confidence.

Our difference

One consistent mentor. The right industry experts when you need them.

Our biggest difference is continuity: you have a mentoring partner who keeps your overall goals in focus, while helping you access specialist support along the way.

We do not run in-house coaching or pretend one person can be an expert in everything. When you need focused help, whether with exam preparation, career exploration, skill development or interviews, we help you connect with relevant industry veterans, specialist mentors and established learning providers.

Together, we work out what you need, when you need it, and what makes sense for your budget. The aim is better guidance, not more services or unnecessary spending.

How we work with you

A clear process, with you in control

  1. Start with an honest assessment

    Understand your strengths, gaps and options without false reassurance.

  2. Build a plan around you

    Bring school choices, exam preparation, profile development and application timelines into one realistic roadmap.

  3. Stay connected

    Use regular check-ins and candid feedback to review progress and adjust your next steps.

  4. Bring in relevant expertise

    Find specialist support when it adds value, with your needs and budget in mind.

  5. Keep you in control

    We guide your thinking and preparation. We do not write your essays or guarantee admissions. Your application, and your decisions, remain yours.

Our mission

To help individuals cut through confusion and make informed education and career choices through honest, personalized mentoring and practical planning, connecting their strengths and aspirations with the skills and opportunities employers need.

Today, our focus is MBA and Master's aspirants. Over time, we aim to bring the same thoughtful guidance to a broader range of education and career decisions.

Our vision

A future where people can make education and career choices with clarity, access the right expertise, and pursue opportunities aligned with their potential and the changing world of work.

Let's start with where you are

You do not need a perfect profile or every answer figured out. We start with an honest conversation about your goals, your options, and what comes next.
